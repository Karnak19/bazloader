---
title: BazLoader
description: An electric airsoft BB loader, driven over Bluetooth.
---

BazLoader upgrades the BULLOADER electric BB loader: it loads an exact number of BBs, counted by
an infrared beam. You set it on the loader itself (a knob and a small screen) or from your phone,
over Bluetooth.

- [Open the app](/bazloader/app/): Chrome on Android, works offline once installed. It shows
  in English or French, following your phone's language; you can change it in the app.
- [BLE protocol](/bazloader/en/protocole-ble/): the technical reference shared by the
  firmware and the app.
